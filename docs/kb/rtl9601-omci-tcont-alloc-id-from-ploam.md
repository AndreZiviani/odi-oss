# An OLT may never set a T-CONT's Alloc-ID over OMCI; the stock stack then binds the PLOAM Alloc-IDs to its T-CONTs in assignment order

ISP1 assigns five Alloc-IDs by PLOAM and points its GEM ports at T-CONTs
0x8000..0x8004 without ever writing their AllocID attribute. The stock stack's
MIB then holds exactly those five Alloc-IDs in 0x8000..0x8004, in the order
they were assigned, and 0x00FF in the rest. ISP2 does set the attribute.

*Last verified: 2026-09-29*

---

## What

G.988 9.2.2 makes attribute 1 of the T-CONT managed entity (class 262) the
link to an Alloc-ID the OLT assigned by the Assign_Alloc-ID PLOAM message, and
the OLT sets it; until then it reads 0x00FF (unassigned, G-PON).

- **ISP2** follows that: its provisioning sets T-CONT 0x8000 to 348 over OMCI
  before the GEM port that uses it (`test/fixtures/omci-session-isp2.txt`).
- **ISP1** sends no Set of class 262 at all in a whole provisioning session
  (`test/fixtures/omci-session-isp1.txt`: no message for class 0x0106), yet
  its five upstream GEM port network CTPs name T-CONTs 0x8000..0x8004.
- The stock stack's MIB on ISP1 (`omcicli mib get 262`) answers 282, 794,
  1050, 1306 and 538 for T-CONTs 0x8000..0x8004 and 255 for the other eleven.
  The Alloc-IDs the same OLT assigned by PLOAM, as our kernel records them in
  arrival order (`alloc_ids` in `/proc/odi_gpon` on ISP1, 2026-09-29), are
  282, 794, 1050, 1306 and 538: the same five, in the same order.

So the stock stack binds the PLOAM-assigned Alloc-IDs to its T-CONT entities
itself, the first to 0x8000 and on in entity-id order, when the OLT does not.

## Why it matters

A table of Alloc-IDs taken from one line is right for that line only: an OLT
that assigns different Alloc-IDs and relies on the ONU's own binding, as ISP1
does, gets T-CONTs that name Alloc-IDs it never assigned, or 0x00FF, which is
refused, and the upstream GEM ports on them are never programmed. omcid binds
from the kernel's list instead (`src/omci/respond/apply_qos.c`, "the T-CONT
Alloc-IDs") and keeps any value the OLT sets.

Binding by position also keeps T-CONT k on Alloc-ID CAM row k, the order the
kernel's upstream queue and flow words were captured in
(`kernel/extra/drivers/net/ethernet/odi/odi_switch_cmd.c`, commands 23 and 25).

## Evidence

- The two session fixtures above, rebuilt from omcid logs of one provisioning
  run of each ISP.
- The stock MIB values: the class 262 rows of `src/omci/generated/omci_defaults.c`
  before this image stopped shipping them (generated by
  `src/omci/tools/mib-defaults.py` from the stock `omcicli mib get all`).
- `/proc/odi_gpon` on ISP1 running this image: `alloc_ids 5 282 794 1050 1306 538`.

Not observed: what the stock stack does when an OLT deallocates an Alloc-ID
(Assign_Alloc-ID type 255) or assigns more than it has T-CONTs; neither ISP
does either.
